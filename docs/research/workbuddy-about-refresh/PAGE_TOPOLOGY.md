# About pages topology

Source pages: `about-intro.html`, `about-history.html`, `about-honor.html`, `about-activity.html`, `capability.html`.

All five routes use the same fixed 70px marketing header, a 300px light About Us banner, a 1200px two-column body (232px existing-style sidebar plus content), and the existing dark marketing footer. At 860px the body becomes one column and the sidebar becomes a horizontally scrollable local navigation. At 760px the banner is 240px tall.

Content models:

- Company introduction: company profile, culture cards, industry-university-research copy.
- Development history: static vertical timeline with year, copy, and archival image.
- Honors: responsive certificate grid.
- Company activities: featured activity rows, activity photo wall, and the source page's media coverage list.
- Capabilities: five linked overview cards leading to the existing local capability detail routes.
