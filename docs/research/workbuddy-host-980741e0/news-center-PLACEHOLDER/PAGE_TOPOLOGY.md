# Page topology

1. Existing site header (out of scope).
2. White news content canvas: generous top spacing, blue 48px accent rule, "新闻中心" heading, and one-line description.
3. Desktop content split: fixed-width rounded category panel on the left; heading/count and 3-column card grid on the right.
4. Each card: rounded image, category/date metadata, two-line title, subtle border and hover lift.
5. Existing site footer (out of scope).

Responsive model: at narrow widths the category panel becomes a horizontal scrollable filter row and the cards collapse from three to two then one column.
