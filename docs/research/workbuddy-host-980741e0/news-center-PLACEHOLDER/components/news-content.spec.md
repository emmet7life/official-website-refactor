# News content component

- White background; content width 1312px at desktop.
- Heading block begins after 108px top padding: 48px by 4px blue rule, dark navy 38px heading, muted 18px description.
- Body uses a 248px category card beside a flexible news column, with 32px gap.
- Category card has a 1px pale blue-gray border, 16px radius, 20px inner padding; selected category is `#165dff` with white text.
- News grid is three equal columns with 24px gaps. Cards have 14px rounded image top corners and a thin `#e5e7eb` border.
- Card image ratio is approximately 1.78:1. Metadata has a pale-blue category tag plus gray date; title is bold dark navy and clamped to two lines.
